Flights, accommodation and other travel products. If a packaged travel itinerary is required, please work with a licensed travel agency.

PASSENGER TRANSFER SUPPORT

Passenger transfers can be arranged through appropriately licensed transportation providers and suitable vehicles, subject to applicable Taiwan regulations and service requirements.

Important clarification

This website is designed to request cycling support services, not to purchase a packaged Taiwan tour. Accommodation, flights, visas, travel insurance and other travel products are not sold by EASY TRAVEL BIKE.

This is a business-positioning statement, not a legal opinion. Specific services and operating arrangements should be reviewed against applicable Taiwan regulations before commercial launch.

Ask us about accommodation, flights, packaged Taiwan tours, itinerary arrangements or other travel-agency services.

HOW THE REFERRAL WORKS

We provide partner information or a referral when appropriate. You contact and contract directly with the licensed travel agency for its travel services.

WHAT EASY TRAVEL BIKE DOES

We remain focused on cycling equipment, bike transport, luggage support, cycling logistics and other cycling-side services.

PARTNER VERIFICATION

Where we list a partner, we aim to identify the travel agency clearly and use current public registration information. Partner status can change and should be confirmed directly.

FAQ · Do you recommend travel agencies?

Yes, when appropriate. If you need travel-agency services beyond cycling support, we may introduce or provide contact information for a licensed travel agency partner. Travel services are discussed, contracted and paid for directly with that travel agency. EASY TRAVEL BIKE does not sell the travel-agency package on its behalf.

For a production website, each listed agency should be verified against the Taiwan Tourism Administration's current travel-agency records before publication and periodically thereafter.

FAQ

Questions Before You Ride.

Do I need to rent a bike?

No. Overseas riders can bring their own bikes and request local support such as bike transport, luggage transfer, passenger transfer support, support vehicles and mechanical assistance.

Can you support small VIP groups?

Yes. The service model is designed to be flexible for small groups and customized cycling programs.

Can overseas cycling clubs work with you directly?

Yes. The B2B pathway is designed for cycling clubs, bike shops, travel agencies and tour operators.

Can the itinerary be customized?

Yes. Route, dates, rider count and service combinations can be discussed as part of the inquiry.
